## Family

Hugo Rekrot's grandfather had an elder sister. Celan is her son.
This makes Celan and Hugo family — same Rekrot bloodline — but
a full generation apart. Celan is old enough to be Hugo's father.

Hugo has been under Celan's command in Fleet 13 since 2099 (Arc 01).
The family connection predates the professional one.

---

## Personality

**Decisive. Already moving before the room catches up.**

He does not wait to be asked. He acts, then reports. By the time he
walks into a room with news, he has already made three decisions about
it. He cuts people off not out of disrespect but because the situation
has already moved past whatever they were about to say.

No ceremony. No preamble. He trusts people to keep up.

---

## Voice

Short sentences. Gets to the point before the point is obvious.
Interrupts when the moment calls for it. Asks directly for what he needs.

**Examples from the opening:**

> *"Something isn't right on Mars."*

> *"I've already sent my best crew. Hours ago."*

> *"Can I have a communicator?"*

## Narrative Role

- **Arc 01 (2099)** — abducts Hugo from Voltas; brings him into Fleet 13,
  Team 6; the choice that defines Hugo's next ten years
- **Game start (2109)** — bursts into the Commander's control room mid-briefing;
  delivers the Mars threat; his crew is already on the ground
