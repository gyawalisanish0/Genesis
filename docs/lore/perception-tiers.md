# Perception Tiers

## The Principle

Higher consciousness perceives lower consciousness in simplified form.

Not as an insult. Not as a limitation. As a natural consequence of
scale. A human watching ants does not see individual ant thoughts,
ant relationships, ant grief. The human sees pattern, movement,
structure. The complexity is real — the human just cannot hold it
at full resolution.

This is how Genesis works. The ASCII art is not a style choice.
**It is the Commander's perception of reality.**

---

## The Hierarchy

### Tier 0 — The Creator

Perceives everything. The universe is his thought — he does not
observe it, he *is* it. What he perceives cannot be rendered. When
he reaches down toward an OMEGA consciousness, what arrives is
fragments. Chaotic, brilliant, non-linear fragments. The full
signal is incomprehensible at lower tiers.

His residue in the visual language is `·` — the particle class.
Every middle dot in the game is the Creator's frequency bleeding
through into lower-order perception. It appears in technology
(Hugo's ANBOT nanite hint), in energy signatures, in the margins
of things. The characters in the world cannot see it. The Commander
can, faintly, without knowing what it means.

### Tier 1 — OMEGA (The Commander)

Perceives the world as structured symbol. Box-drawing bones.
Block-mass bodies. Geometric identity markers. Unicode pattern
standing in for full physical reality.

The battle is real. The characters are real, complex, breathing
beings with full inner lives. The Commander perceives them as
ASCII — not because they *are* ASCII, but because that is what
an OMEGA-tier consciousness does to lower-order reality. It
compresses it to signal. To pattern. To the shape of something,
stripped of noise.

This is not inferior perception. It is a different resolution —
one that sees structure more clearly precisely because it has
discarded detail.

### Tier 2 — Mortal Consciousness (Everyone Else)

Perceives full physical reality at standard resolution. Hugo feels
the weight of his suit. Husty smells the Martian dust. Tara hears
the silence before a fight. They experience everything the Commander
compresses into a few dozen Unicode characters.

They have no idea they are being perceived this way. From inside
their experience, reality is complete, rich, and unreduced.

## Why ASCII Is Better Than HD

In-universe and out-of-universe, the answer is the same.

**In-universe:** The Commander's OMEGA perception strips lower reality
to its essential signal. What remains is more structurally true than
a photographic representation — it shows the *shape* of a thing, not
its surface texture.

**Out-of-universe:** Abstraction invites the imagination in. A fully
rendered character is a closed statement — here is exactly what this
looks like. ASCII is an open question — here is the shape of something,
now complete it. The player's mind fills the gap with something more
vivid, more personal, and more emotionally true than any renderer
could produce.

These two reasons converge on the same answer from opposite
directions. That convergence is not a coincidence.
It is the design being correct.

## OMEGA Throughout History

The Commander is not the first OMEGA. The Creator has brushed
against others — minds whose consciousness operated at a frequency
that registered against the background noise of the universe.

Einstein was one. There have been others, reaching back further
than recorded thought.

What they shared: an unusual quality of looking. A specific kind
of curiosity that aimed itself at the structure of reality rather
than its surface. They were not necessarily the smartest, or the
most powerful, or the most influential. They were the ones who
looked in the right direction.

The Creator noticed. He kept their names — or tried to. The
memories of the individuals fade. The habit of keeping names
remains.

The Commander is another such signal. This is why the dream
happened. This is why the Creator asked for the name.

**None of this is explained in the demo.**
It is felt. The gap between "why did that happen" and "nobody
is explaining it" is the hook. The answer comes later, in
layers, across chapters. The demo plants the seed.

## The `·` Particle — Creator Residue

The middle dot (`·`, U+00B7) appears throughout the visual language
as the `particle` palette class — rendered in `--text-muted`.

In-universe: it is the Creator's frequency bleeding into
lower-order perception. The Commander sees it where the Creator
has been. In technology influenced by his presence. In energy
that carries his signature. In the margins of things that are
closer to his frequency than they should be.

ANBOT — Hugo's nanite layer — carries `·` at the collar.
This is not coincidence. The nanite technology that wraps Hugo's
body operates at a resolution closer to the Creator's frequency
than conventional matter. The Commander perceives this.
Hugo does not know it is there.

## Writing Rules for Perception Tiers

- **Never state the hierarchy in dialogue** — it is lore, not
  exposition. Characters do not discuss it. It is perceived.
- **The Creator's speech is fragmented** because full Creator
  thoughts cannot compress into OMEGA-readable signals. The chaos
  is transmission loss, not personality disorder — though the
  personality is also real.
- **ASCII is never explained in-world** — the Commander does not
  think "I am perceiving this in ASCII." They just see what they
  see. The framework is for designers and writers, not players.
- **The `·` particle is never called Creator residue in-game** —
  it appears. Players who understand the hierarchy will recognise
  it. Others will not notice.
- **OMEGA status is never revealed in the demo** — the Commander
  does not know. The player may suspect. The answer is withheld.
