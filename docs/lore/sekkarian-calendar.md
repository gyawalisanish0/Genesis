# Sekkarian Calendar System

## Overview

Sekkarians use a planetary orbital calendar — the same conceptual basis as
Earth's system, anchored to their planet's rotation and orbit around their star.
The numbers are close enough to Earth standard that cross-civilisation
communication is straightforward, but distinct enough to matter across a
lifetime.

> **Status:** rough estimate — values are locked for the demo and can be
> refined with more precision in later chapters.

---

## Base Units

| Unit | Value |
|---|---|
| 1 Sekkar day | 26.42 Earth hours |
| 1 Sekkar year | 348 Sekkar days |
| 1 Sekkar year | 9,194.16 Earth hours |

---

## Conversion

| Direction | Factor |
|---|---|
| SK years → Earth years | × 1.0488 |
| Earth years → SK years | × 0.9534 |

**1 Sekkar year ≈ 1.049 Earth years**

The difference is small per year but accumulates. A Sekkarian who has lived
60 SK years is almost 63 in Earth reckoning — not dramatic, but enough to
matter when establishing seniority, generational gaps, or legal age thresholds
across civilisations.

## Writing Convention

When writing ages in narrative or character docs, use Sekkar years for
Sekkarian characters with an Earth equivalent in parentheses:

> *Celan Rekrot Syfhernyle — 58 SK (≈ 61 Earth years)*

For human characters, use Earth years only unless the context is explicitly
cross-civilisational (a treaty, a medical record shared between fleets, etc.).

When a character's age is uncertain or deliberately vague, use approximate
ranges rather than exact figures:

> *"Mid-fifties by human reckoning."*

---

## Notes

- The Sekkarian calendar has no documented month system yet — days and years
  only for now; months can be added in a later chapter
- No universal "standard year" exists in this universe; each civilisation uses
  its own and conversion is handled case by case
- The slight length difference means Sekkar seasons are marginally longer than
  Earth seasons — relevant for any lore involving Sekkar's climate or agriculture
